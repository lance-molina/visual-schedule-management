# Research paper

> Required deliverable, graded at 25% of the final project. Citation style: **IEEE** (confirm with the instructor).
> Chapter 2 contains only other people's work; Chapter 4 contains only your own data. Plagiarism is a failing grade.

## Format

PDF, A4, 1-inch margins, 12 pt Times New Roman or Arial, double-spaced. Total minimum 15 to 20 pages
(the chapter minimums below add up to 15 to 21, so aim for 18 to 20 and confirm the exact rule).
Submit the PDF and the source (`.docx` or `.md`) to both GitHub and Brightspace.

## Files to create here

- `paper.docx` or `paper.md` (source) and `paper.pdf` (final)
- `references.md` or a spreadsheet of sources you actually opened (authors, year, title, venue, DOI/URL,
  finding used, section/page, verified on the publisher page)

## Structure and minimum pages

| Part | Content | Min pages |
|------|---------|-----------|
| Title page | Project title, student names, course, date | 1 |
| Abstract | Summary of the project, 150 to 300 words (write last) | 1 |
| Chapter 1: Introduction | Background, problem statement, objectives, significance, scope | 2 to 3 |
| Chapter 2: Review of Related Literature | HCI, assistive technology, visual schedules, usability evaluation | 3 to 4 |
| Chapter 3: Methodology | Research design, system development, usability evaluation | 2 to 3 |
| Chapter 4: Results and Discussion | System features (with screenshots), usability test results, findings | 2 to 3 |
| Chapter 5: Conclusion and Recommendations | Summary, limitations, future work | 1 to 2 |
| References | IEEE format | 1 |
| Appendices | User manual, SUS questionnaire, screenshots | 2 to 3 |

Chapter 4 must include screenshots (fictional data only) and the real SUS scores, task completion
rates and user feedback.

For Chapter 5 (limitations), use the "Known limitations" list in the root `README.md` and
`../technical-documentation/design-decisions.md`.

## Rules for this paper

- Never invent references, participants, scores or quotes.
- Leave a visible `[PENDING: real data]` marker until real results exist.
- Open every source yourself before citing it.
