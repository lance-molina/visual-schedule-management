# Research paper

> Citation style: **APA 7th edition** (confirmed by the instructor).
> Scope was reduced by the instructor: the paper covers the title page, abstract, Chapters 1 to 3 and the references.
> Chapter 2 contains only other people's work. Plagiarism is a failing grade.

## Format

PDF, A4, 1-inch margins, 12 pt Times New Roman or Arial, double-spaced.
Submit the PDF and the source (`.docx` or `.md`) to both GitHub and Brightspace.

## Files to create here

- `paper.docx` or `paper.md` (source) and `paper.pdf` (final)
- `references.md` or a spreadsheet of sources you actually opened (authors, year, title, venue, DOI/URL,
  finding used, section/page, verified on the publisher page)

## Structure

| Part | Content |
|------|---------|
| Title page | Project title, student names, course, date |
| Abstract | Summary of the project, 150 to 300 words (write last) |
| Chapter 1: Introduction | Background, problem statement, objectives, significance, scope |
| Chapter 2: Review of Related Literature | HCI, assistive technology, visual schedules, accessibility |
| Chapter 3: Methodology | Research design, system development, system testing |
| References | APA 7th edition |

Chapters 4 and 5, the appendices and the usability study are not required.
For the limitations and future work, use the "Known limitations" list in the root `README.md` and
`../technical-documentation/design-decisions.md`.

## Rules for this paper

- Never invent references or results.
- Open every source yourself before citing it.
